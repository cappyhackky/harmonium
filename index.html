    How to use the harmonium

    Playing

    • Mouse: press a key, or press and drag across keys to glide.
    • Touch: use several fingers for chords. Swipe sideways on the keys to scroll the keyboard.
    • Computer keys: the Z row (Z X C V B N M , . /) plays white keys, and S D G H J L ; play the black keys above them. The Q row (Q to P) is one octave higher, with 2 3 5 6 7 9 0 for black keys.
    • ← → or the Low / High switch moves those rows to another octave.

    Key labels

    • सा रे ग: sargam names. A dot under a key means the lower octave (mandra), a dot above means the upper (taar).
    • Black keys are the komal (flat) notes in small letters: re, ga, dha, ni. Ma′ is the sharp Ma.
    • C D E: the real note each key sounds. It follows the Scale changer.
    • Z X C: the computer key that plays each key.

    Bellows and air

    Auto keeps the air steady, and the Air slider sets how strong it is: more air is louder and brighter.

    Manual works like the real thing. Air drains while you play. Hold Space or the Pump button, or drag the bellows up and down, to refill. Low air sounds quiet, dull and slightly flat.

    Reed stops and sound

    • Bass 16′ sounds an octave lower, Main 8′ at normal pitch, Tenor 4′ an octave higher.
    • Celeste is a second 8′ reed tuned slightly apart, which gives the slow shimmering beat.
    • One reed always stays pulled. A change applies to the next notes you play.
    • Volume, Tone (darker to brighter) and Room (echo) shape the sound.

    Drone

    Sa Pa, Sa Ma or Sa Ni holds a steady background pair of notes while you play, like a tanpura. It follows your Scale changer and uses a little air in Manual mode.

    Scale changer

    Choose which pitch is Sa. Every key shifts together, so the sargam names stay in place. The names are the ones used on Indian harmoniums: Safed 1 is C, Kali 1 is C♯, Safed 2 is D, and so on. Pick the one that sits well for the singer.

    Song guide

    • Choose Ghazals or Qawwali, then a song. Picking a song lights its raag on the keyboard; switch that off with Light the keys or the × beside the keyboard.
    • Hear ascent and descent plays the raag’s basic run with the current note marked, so you can follow along.
    • Taal practice gives a clap and wave count with a click. Start it, set the tempo, and clap on the marked beats.
    • Apply a starting sound sets reeds, tone, room and the Sa Pa drone for a ghazal or a qawwali. Change anything afterwards.
    • When you scroll down to play, a slim bar pins to the top with the raag notes and beat counter, so you never lose your place. Hide guide folds the panel away.
    • Songs marked “Find the raag by ear” have no raag confirmed in the sources I checked, so nothing is lit for them.

    Good to know

    • The first key press or tap wakes the sound. If you hear nothing, check your device volume and silent switch.
